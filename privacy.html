Privacy Policy – Guntha Bigha: Land Converter

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Guntha Bigha: Land Converter ("the app") uses and why. The app is made by NEEL PAVALE ("we"). Contact: neelpavale7@gmail.com.

The app is not affiliated with any government. Land unit sizes in the app are reference values; confirm with official land records.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Saved plots, recent conversions, your own units and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Backups you create are files you choose where to send (for example to WhatsApp or Google Drive). We never receive them.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you use:

  • Walk with GPS – to record the corners of your plot, and
  • automatic state/district detection in the unit converter.

Corner positions are stored on your phone with your saved plots. To find your state and district, the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service (provided by Google on Android), which receives your approximate position for that lookup. You can deny or turn off location permission at any time; the rest of the app still works.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Other services

  • Google Fonts – the app may download fonts from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Links you open (WhatsApp sharing, the Mahabhumi land-records site) are handled by those apps and websites under their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Selling data

We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
