Guntha Bigha: Land Converter

Guntha · Bigha · Acre · Sq ft  |  गुंठा · बीघा · एकड़

What the app does
  • Converts land units for every Indian state – guntha, bigha, katha, cent, marla, kanal, acre, hectare and more.
  • Measures any plot: type its sides, walk around it with GPS, or use quick shapes.
  • Shows the plot's shape and area in every unit, and shares it on WhatsApp or as a PDF.
  • Works in English, Hindi and Marathi, and works offline.
